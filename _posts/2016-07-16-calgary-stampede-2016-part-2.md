This is the 2nd and final part of my write-up and pictures from the 2016 Calgary Stampede parade. It is also the last day of the 2016 edition of the Stampede. I love shooting colourful outdoor scenes in good light with no flash. The parade provided me with this opportunity. I also think the Nikkor 70–200 f/2.8 lens rocks. It is like silk. [Part 1 of this series]({{ site.url }}/blog/calgary-stampede-2016-part-1/) can be found if you missed it.
