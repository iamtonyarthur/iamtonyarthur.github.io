'Twas fun visiting "the most magical place on earth" — Disney World's Magic Kingdom Park in Orlando, Florida. It was one of three theme parks that my family and I visited. We also visited Epcot and Animal Kingdom. We spent one day in each park and needed one day of rest. The remaining days were spent shopping and traveling. Eight days go by really fast.
